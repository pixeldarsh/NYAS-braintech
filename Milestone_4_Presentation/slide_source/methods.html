Acoustic track

Varying tissue properties

Fair testing: each person held out in turn, then a frozen model tested once on 3 new people

Joining the tracks: a software-only test of how often a hypothetical trigger would be mistimed

Tools: Python, scikit-learn, k-Wave (Treeby and Cox, 2010). Software only.

On the EEG side we extracted frequency features, trained a random forest and compared it with simple baselines. To test fairly, each person was held out in turn, and then we froze the model and tested it once on three new people. On the acoustic side we simulated a beam through a layered virtual head, checked the code against exact solutions, and varied tissue properties. Finally we joined the tracks with a software test of trigger timing.
