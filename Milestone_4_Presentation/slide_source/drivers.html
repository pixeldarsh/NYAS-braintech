We varied tissue properties together across ranges we assumed around published values. Bone absorption and skull thickness had the largest effect. The estimated focal pressure ranged from about 0.22 to 0.52 of the water value. We stopped early with 19 of 32 samples, and because the unfinished ones had more absorption, this range comes from a fitted estimate. We also checked our code against exact solutions, and those checks caught a real bug. Testing against known answers really matters.
