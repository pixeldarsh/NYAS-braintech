Results: what reaches the target

About a third of the focal pressure remains

In water: focal pressure 1.00

Through skin, skull and brain: 0.34

Treating the skull as one solid layer overestimates focal pressure by about 50% (0.51 vs 0.34).

Absorption matters: without it, the 3-layer model gives 0.54.

2D simulation, uncalibrated, 500 kHz. Pressure is relative to the same source in water. Not a safety or dose value.

Here is our simulated beam. On the left, in water, it builds a strong focus at the target, marked with a cross. On the right, it passes through skin, a three-layer skull and brain. Part of the wave bounces back, part is absorbed, and the focus is about one-third as strong. Modelling the skull as a single solid layer would overestimate this by about 50 percent. These are simulated relative values, not safety or dose values.
